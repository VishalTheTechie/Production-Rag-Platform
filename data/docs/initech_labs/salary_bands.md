## 3. Annual Incentive Plan (AIP) & Bonus Rules

The Annual Incentive Plan rewards individual performance and corporate goal achievement. 

### Target Bonus Percentages
* **L1:** 5% of base salary (Max cap: 7.5%)
* **L2:** 10% of base salary (Max cap: 15.0%)
* **L3:** 15% of base salary (Max cap: 22.5%)
* **L4:** 20% of base salary (Max cap: 30.0%)
* **L5:** 30% of base salary (Max cap: 45.0%)
* **L6:** 40% of base salary (Max cap: 60.0%)

### Calculation Formula
$$\text{Final Bonus Payout} = \text{Base Salary} \times \text{Target \%} \times \text{Individual Rating Multiplier} \times \text{Corporate Multiplier}$$

### Performance Multipliers
During annual reviews, managers assign a score from 1.0 to 5.0:
* **Rating 1.0–2.4 (Unsatisfactory):** 0% multiplier
* **Rating 2.5–2.9 (Needs Improvement):** 50% multiplier
* **Rating 3.0–3.7 (Meets Expectations):** 100% multiplier
* **Rating 3.8–4.4 (Exceeds Expectations):** 125% multiplier
* **Rating 4.5–5.0 (Exceptional):** 150% multiplier

### Corporate Multiplier Rules
The Corporate Multiplier is determined by the Board of Directors based on IND filing achievements and runway targets. It ranges from **0.8x to 1.2x**. For FY2025, the baseline assumptions assume a **1.0x** corporate multiplier.

### Prorating & Cut-Off Dates
* **Eligibility Cut-Off:** Employees hired on or before **September 30, 2025**, are eligible for a prorated bonus based on months active. Employees hired after September 30, 2025, are ineligible for the FY2025 AIP cycle.
* **Payout Date:** Annual bonuses are distributed on **March 15, 2026**. Employees must be actively employed at Initech Labs on the payout date to receive their bonus.

## 4. Special Incentive Provisions

### Patent Milestone Award
Dr. Chloe Bennett (Chief Technology Officer) must sign off on all patent bonuses. Upon formal granting of a US Patent where Initech Labs is the primary assignee:
* **Lead Inventor:** $5,000 one-time cash award.
* **Co-Inventors:** $2,500 split equally among co-inventors (minimum payout of $1,000 per person).

### On-Call Weekend Shift Differential
L1 and L2 lab staff assigned to mandatory weekend cell culture monitoring receive a flat differential of **$350 per weekend shift** (Saturday or Sunday).

### Relocation & Signing Bonus Clawback
Signing bonuses over **$10,000** carry a mandatory 24-month repayment schedule:
* Resignation within 0–12 months: 100% repayment required.
* Resignation within 13–24 months: 50% repayment required.
