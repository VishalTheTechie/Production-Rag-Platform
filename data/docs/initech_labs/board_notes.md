### 2. Q3 2024 Financial Performance Review
Marcus Thorne presented the Q3 financial report:
*   **Q3 Revenue:** $4.2 million, representing a 12% decline relative to Q2 2024, driven by milestone payment delays from the Novartis licensing agreement.
*   **YTD Revenue:** $14.8 million.
*   **FY2024 Target Revision:** Adjusted downward from $22.0 million to $18.5 million.
*   **Cash Position:** $31.5 million in cash and cash equivalents. Current net burn rate is $2.25 million per month, yielding approximately 14 months of remaining runway without structural adjustments.

---

### 3. Planned Acquisition: Project Titan (BioVector Dynamics)
Dr. Vance presented the finalized non-binding Letter of Intent (LOI) to acquire BioVector Dynamics, an early-stage viral vector platform developer.
*   **Transaction Valuation:** $45.0 million total consideration ($20.0 million in cash; $25.0 million in Initech Series C preferred stock).
*   **Target Milestones:** Financial due diligence completion by November 20, 2024; definitive agreement execution target of December 15, 2024; formal transaction closing target of January 15, 2025. Exclusivity expires on November 30, 2024.
*   **Governance Rule:** Pursuant to Article VI, Section 3 of the Bylaws, any capital commitment exceeding $25.0 million requires unanimous approval of the Board.
*   **Board Action:** Following discussion, a motion to authorize Dr. Vance to execute the LOI and proceed to definitive legal agreements was proposed by David Miller, seconded by Sarah Lin, and **passed unanimously (5-0)**.

---

### 4. Restructuring Plan: "Project Streamline" (Layoff Plan)
To optimize operating cash burn and fund the integration of BioVector Dynamics, Marcus Thorne presented a workforce reduction plan:
*   **Total Headcount Reduction:** 35 full-time employees (FTEs), representing 18% of the total 194-person workforce.
*   **Affected Units:** Diagnostics Division (20 FTEs; division to be sunset), General & Administrative (10 FTEs), Quality Assurance (5 FTEs). R&D and Vector Engineering teams are explicitly exempt.
*   **Execution Schedule:** Individual notifications will occur on Tuesday, November 12, 2024. Separation date set for November 26, 2024.
*   **Severance & Terms:** 8 weeks baseline severance pay, plus 2 weeks per year of service; full COBRA medical coverage through May 31, 2025. 
*   **Financial Impact:** Upfront severance outlay estimated at $1.85 million; total projected annualized payroll savings of $6.4 million.
*   **Board Action:** Motion to approve Project Streamline moved by Sarah Lin, seconded by David Miller. **Passed 4-1** (Dr. Sterling dissenting, citing risk to QA compliance timelines).
