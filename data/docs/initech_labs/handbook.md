## 1. Working Hours & Attendance
Initech Labs operates standard business hours from **8:30 AM to 5:00 PM EST**, Monday through Friday. 

* **Core Hours:** All full-time employees must be reachable and available for meetings during core hours between **10:00 AM and 3:00 PM EST**.
* **Rest & Meal Breaks:** Employees working a full shift are entitled to a **45-minute unpaid lunch break** and two paid **15-minute rest breaks**.
* **Lab Protocol:** Wet Lab personnel (Department 40) must physically log entry and exit times using their BioAccess Keycard system by **9:00 AM** daily. 
* **Overtime:** Non-exempt employees require written pre-approval from **Dr. Marcus Vance** prior to working more than 40 hours in a given workweek.

---

## 2. Paid Time Off (PTO) & Leave Policy
Full-time employees accrue PTO at a rate of **1.66 days per month**, totaling **20 accrued PTO days** per calendar year.

* **PTO Carry-Over:** A maximum of **5 unused PTO days** may be carried over into the next calendar year. Any additional accrued, unused PTO beyond 5 days will expire automatically on **January 31st**.
* **Sick Leave:** Employees receive **8 dedicated paid sick days** per year, granted upfront on **January 1st**. Sick leave does not roll over from year to year.
* **Bereavement Leave:** Employees are eligible for up to **5 consecutive paid days** for the loss of an immediate family member and **2 paid days** for extended family.
* **Notice Requirement:** Any planned PTO request exceeding **3 consecutive business days** must be submitted through the Workday portal at least **14 calendar days** in advance.

---

## 3. Remote & Flexible Work Policy
Initech Labs enforces a tiered remote work framework based on role requirements:

* **Tier A (Wet Lab / Sample Management):** On-site required 5 days/week. Zero remote work days allowed.
* **Tier B (Bioinformatics / Data Science):** Hybrid model. Mandatory on-site attendance on **Tuesdays and Thursdays**; remote work permitted on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays.
* **Tier C (Administration & Operations):** Fully remote option available following the successful completion of a **90-day probationary period**.
* **Equipment & Security:** Remote employees receive a one-time **$600 work-from-home stipend** on their **30th day of employment**. Remote workers must connect exclusively through the Initech Cisco AnyConnect VPN and undergo Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) re-authorization every **12 hours**.

## 4. Code of Conduct & Lab Ethics

* **Confidentiality:** Employees must sign Form B-12 (Proprietary Data Agreement). Unauthorized dissemination of Proprietary Genomic Data (PGD) will result in immediate termination of employment and a liquidated damages penalty of **$10,000**.
* **Dress Code:** Casual attire is permitted in administrative offices. Personnel entering Biosafety Level 2 (BSL-2) areas must strictly wear natural-fiber long pants, closed-toe non-porous shoes, and required PPE (lab coat, safety goggles, and nitrile gloves).
* **Substance Policy:** Initech Labs maintains a zero-tolerance policy for illicit drugs and alcohol on company premises. Mandatory random drug screenings are managed quarterly by BioSafety Officer **Elena Rostova**.
* **Ethics Reporting:** Violations of company policy or research integrity should be reported immediately to the Ethics Hotline at **1-800-555-INIT (Ext. 99)** or emailed to `ethics@initechlabs.io`. The Ethics Committee reviews all submission logs within **48 hours**.
