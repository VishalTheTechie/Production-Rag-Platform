# Initech Labs Customer FAQ

### 1. What is Initech Labs and what services do you offer?
Founded on March 14, 2022, by CEO Marcus Sterling and Chief Scientific Officer Dr. Evelyn Vance, Initech Labs is a direct-to-consumer biotechnology company specializing in epigenetic and metabolic health profiling. Our flagship products include the **GenePulse™ 360 Kit** ($299), which analyzes 45+ genetic markers for longevity and metabolic efficiency, and the **MetaboTrack Pro** monthly biomarker monitoring service ($49/month).

### 2. How do I properly collect and prepare my saliva sample for the GenePulse™ 360 Kit?
To ensure accurate sequencing:
* Do not eat, drink, smoke, or chew gum for **30 minutes** prior to sample collection.
* Fasting for **8 hours** before collection is recommended for optimal metabolic biomarker clarity.
* Fill the collection vial with saliva until it reaches the **2.0 mL** fill line (excluding bubbles).
* Close the cap tightly to release the stabilization buffer liquid, then invert the tube gently **10 times** to mix.

### 3. What are the shipping deadlines and sample stability limits?
Saliva samples mixed with our proprietary stabilization solution remain stable at room temperature (68°F–77°F) for up to **7 days**. Samples must be dropped off at a designated FedEx location within **48 hours** of collection using the prepaid shipping envelope included in your kit. Do not freeze the sample unless instructed by customer support.

### 4. How long does it take to receive my test results?
Once your sample arrives at our laboratory in Austin, Texas, standard processing takes **14 business days**. You will receive an automated email alert when your interactive health dashboard is ready at `portal.initechlabs.com`.

### 5. What is the policy for cancelling or modifying my MetaboTrack Pro subscription?
MetaboTrack Pro subscriptions automatically bill on the **15th of each month**. To avoid being charged for the upcoming cycle, subscription cancellations or modifications must be submitted through your account settings by **11:59 PM EST on the 10th of the month** (at least 5 calendar days prior to billing). 

### 6. What are the age restrictions for Initech Labs testing services?
* **Adults (18+):** May purchase and submit samples independently.
* **Minors (Ages 13–17):** Eligible for testing only with a completed **Form IG-9 (Parental Consent Authorization)**, signed by a parent or legal guardian.
* **Children under 13:** We currently do not process samples for individuals under 13 years of age.

### 7. What is your refund policy?
We offer a full refund (minus a **$12.99** original shipping charge) on unopened kits returned within **30 calendar days** of the delivery date. If the outer kit box seal has been broken, a **$15.00 restocking and processing fee** will be deducted from your total refund. Once a sample has arrived at our laboratory and processing has begun, no refunds will be issued.

### 8. How does the complimentary Genetic Consultation work?
Every GenePulse™ 360 purchase includes one complimentary **30-minute telehealth consultation** led by Lead Genetic Counselor Sarah Jenkins or a member of her team. Consultations must be scheduled within **90 calendar days** from the date your final report is generated. Unused consultation credits expire after 90 days.

### 9. Is my genetic data secure and kept private?
Yes. Initech Labs strictly complies with HIPAA standards and uses **AES-256 encryption** for all stored data. We never sell, rent, or share individual genetic data with third parties, insurance providers, or employers. De-identified, anonymized data is only used for internal clinical validation if you explicitly opt-in via **Section 4 of the Digital Terms of Service**.
