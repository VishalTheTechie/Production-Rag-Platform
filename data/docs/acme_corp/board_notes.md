### 1. Q3 Financial Review & Q4 2024 Projections

CFO Marcus Vance presented the Q3 financial results for Acme Corp's project management software division. 

*   **Q3 Revenue:** Acme Corp generated **$24.8 million** in revenue for Q3 2024 (a 12% increase YoY), missing the internal target of **$26.5 million**.
*   **Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR):** Reached **$96.2 million** as of September 30, 2024.
*   **Margins & Churn:** Gross margin stood at 74%. Net revenue retention was 101%, impacted by a 4.2% churn rate in the mid-market account segment.
*   **Q4 Guidance:** The board approved a updated Q4 revenue target of **$25.5 million to $26.2 million**, aiming to close the year at **$99.0 million ARR**.

### 2. Strategic Acquisition: "Project Nexus" (TaskFlow Inc.)

CEO Sarah Jenkins detailed the proposed acquisition of **TaskFlow Inc.**, an Austin-based developer of AI-driven resource allocation software.

*   **Purchase Price:** **$18.5 million** total valuation structured as **$11.1 million in cash** (60%) and **$7.4 million in Acme restricted stock** (40%).
*   **Retention Pool:** $1.2 million allocated for TaskFlow key engineering personnel over a 24-month vesting period. TaskFlow CEO **Liam O'Connor** will join Acme as Vice President of AI Integration.
*   **Acquisition Timeline:**
    *   *November 4, 2024:* Signing of binding Letter of Intent (LOI).
    *   *December 12, 2024:* Completion of financial and technical due diligence.
    *   *January 15, 2025:* Formal deal closing date.
*   **Board Action:** Unanimously approved by all board members present.

### 3. Restructuring Plan: "Project Velocity" (Reduction in Force)

To offset acquisition integration costs and reallocate budget toward generative AI development, CFO Marcus Vance and CEO Sarah Jenkins presented a headcount reduction plan.

*   **Impact Scope:** **12% reduction** of total workforce (65 employees out of 540 total headcount).
*   **Affected Departments:** Customer Success (30% cut), Marketing (20% cut), Operations (15% cut), and Legacy Engineering (15% cut). Enterprise Sales and Core AI R&D teams are **100% exempt**.
*   **Execution Schedule:**
    *   *November 11, 2024:* Executive and manager briefing.
    *   *November 14, 2024:* Employee notifications delivered via 1-on-1 meetings.
    *   *November 29, 2024:* Official final separation date.

#### Selection Rules & Severance Terms:
1. **Rule 1 (Performance):** Employees receiving an annual performance evaluation score below 3.0 (out of 5.0) in both 2022 and 2023 review cycles will be prioritized for selection.
2. **Rule 2 (Redundancy):** Roles duplicated by TaskFlow’s automated platform infrastructure will be phased out, regardless of employee tenure.
3. **Severance Package:** Severance consists of 8 weeks base pay, plus 1 additional week of pay per full year of tenure beyond 2 years of service (capped at 16 weeks maximum).
4. **Healthcare Support:** Company-sponsored COBRA coverage extended through **February 28, 2025**.
5. **Covenants:** Non-compete clauses are waived for all non-executive impacted employees; non-disclosure and non-disparagement agreements remain fully enforced.
