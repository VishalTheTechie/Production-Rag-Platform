# Acme Corp Employee Handbook
**Document Version:** 4.2  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**HR Contact:** Sarah Jenkins, VP of People Operations (sjenkins@acmecorp.com)

Welcome to Acme Corp, creators of *PlanIt Online*, the industry-leading project management platform. This handbook outlines key workplace policies for all full-time employees.

## 1. Working Hours & Attendance
* **Standard Schedule:** The standard workweek is 40 hours, Monday through Friday. Standard operating hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM EST.
* **Core Hours:** All team members must be available online or in-office during Core Hours from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM EST for mandatory meetings and real-time collaboration.
* **Overtime:** Non-exempt employees must obtain written approval from Operations Director David Ross before working more than 40 hours in a single workweek. Pre-approved overtime is paid at 1.5 times the standard hourly rate.

## 2. Leave Policy
* **Paid Time Off (PTO):** Full-time staff accrue 1.5 PTO days per month, totaling 18 days annually. Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused PTO days into the next calendar year; these carried-over days must be used by March 31 or they will be forfeited.
* **Holidays:** Acme Corp recognizes 10 fixed company holidays per year, along with 2 Floating Holidays allocated to each employee on January 1.
* **Sick Days:** Employees receive 5 dedicated paid sick days per year, refreshing annually on January 1. Unused sick days do not roll over to the following year and are not paid out upon separation.
* **Parental Leave:** Employees with at least 12 months of continuous service are eligible for 12 weeks of fully paid primary caregiver leave or 4 weeks of fully paid secondary caregiver leave following the birth or placement of an adopted child.

## 3. Remote Work & Hybrid Policy
* **Hybrid Schedule:** Headquarters staff based in Austin, TX are required to work in the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays are designated optional remote work days.
* **Stipend:** Active full-time employees receive a $75/month tax-free remote workspace allowance, credited automatically on the last paycheck of each month.
* **Equipment:** Acme Corp provides a standard hardware package consisting of a 16-inch MacBook Pro (or Dell XPS 15) and one 27-inch monitor. Additional equipment requests must be submitted through IT Specialist Mark Thorne.
* **Absence Notification:** If working from an unapproved remote location or taking an unscheduled remote day, employees must post a notification in the `#attendance` Slack channel by 8:30 AM EST.

## 4. Code of Conduct & Compliance
* **Internal Software Usage:** Employees must strictly use *PlanIt Online* for all task management, sprint tracking, and project documentation. The use of competing software (e.g., Asana, Jira) for company business is strictly prohibited.
* **Gifts & Gratuities:** Employees may not accept gifts, favors, or entertainment valued over $50 from clients, vendors, or prospective business partners.
* **Conflicts of Interest:** Outside employment, freelance consulting, or advisory roles require prior written authorization from General Counsel Elena Rostova.
* **Disciplinary Framework:** Policy violations follow a three-stage progressive resolution process: (1) Verbal Warning, (2) Written Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) lasting 30 days, and (3) Employment Termination.
