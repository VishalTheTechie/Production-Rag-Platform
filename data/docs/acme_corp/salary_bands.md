### 1. Overview & Scope
This document outlines the base salary ranges, target bonuses, equity guidelines, and bonus payout rules for Acme Corp (Project Management SaaS Division). Compensation bands apply to all full-time employees across North America and are reviewed annually against B2B SaaS industry benchmarks.

---

### 2. Job Levels & Compensation Bands

Base salary midpoints are adjusted by department: **Engineering and Product** roles apply a **1.10x multiplier** to base midpoints; **Corporate/G&A and Customer Success** use standard base ranges. **Sales/GTM** operates on a 50/50 On-Target Earnings (OTE) split.

*Note: For L3 Account Executives, OTE is set at $240,000 ($120,000 base / $120,000 commission) tied to a $1,200,000 Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) quota.*

### 3. Annual Bonus Mechanics & Formula

Annual cash bonuses are calculated using the following formula:
$$\text{Actual Bonus} = \text{Base Salary} \times \text{Target Bonus \%} \times \text{Company Performance Multiplier} \times \text{Individual Factor}$$

#### A. Company Performance Multiplier (FY2024 ARR Goals)
Approved by the Board of Directors on December 12, 2023:
* **Below Threshold (< $42.0M ARR):** 0% multiplier (No bonus pool funded)
* **Threshold ($42.0M – $47.9M ARR):** 75% multiplier
* **Target ($48.0M ARR):** 100% multiplier
* **Stretch Maximum ($54.0M+ ARR):** 150% maximum multiplier

#### B. Individual Performance Rating Multiplier
Evaluated during annual reviews completed by managers by January 24, 2025:
* **Rating 1 (Unsatisfactory):** 0% multiplier
* **Rating 2 (Needs Improvement):** 50% multiplier
* **Rating 3 (Meets Expectations):** 100% multiplier
* **Rating 4 (Exceeds Expectations):** 125% multiplier
* **Rating 5 (Exceptional):** 150% multiplier

### 4. Eligibility & Payout Rules

1. **Hire Date Cutoff:** Employees hired on or after **October 1, 2024**, are ineligible for the FY2024 annual bonus cycle. Employees hired between January 1, 2024, and September 30, 2024, will receive a prorated bonus based on days employed.
2. **Payout Date:** Approved FY2024 bonuses will be paid out on **Friday, March 14, 2025**. Active employment on the date of payout is required.
3. **Clawback Clause:** For Sales commissions and variable bonuses overseen by Revenue Operations Lead Tom Hiddleston, a full payout clawback applies if a customer churns within **90 days** of contract execution.
4. **Equity Vesting Schedule:** RSUs vest over 4 years: **25% on the 1-year anniversary** cliff date, followed by **6.25% quarterly** vesting thereafter, managed via the Shareworks platform.
