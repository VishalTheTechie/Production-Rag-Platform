# Acme Corp - AcmeFlow Pro Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. What is AcmeFlow Pro and how do I start a trial?
AcmeFlow Pro is Acme Corp’s flagship cloud-based project management platform designed for mid-size businesses. You can sign up for a 14-day free trial directly on our website without entering credit card details.

### 2. What are the available pricing tiers?
We offer three standard subscription tiers billed per seat:
* **Starter:** $12 per user/month (up to 10 team members).
* **Business:** $28 per user/month (unlimited users, includes Gantt charts and workload management).
* **Enterprise:** $45 per user/month (includes custom SSO, dedicated account manager, and audit logs).

### 3. Are discounts available for non-profits or annual commitments?
Yes. Annual subscriptions receive an automatic 15% discount across all tiers. Registered non-profit organizations qualify for a 20% discount on Business and Enterprise plans upon submitting IRS Form 501(c)(3) documentation to billing@acmecorp.io.

### 4. What is your cancellation and refund policy?
Acme Corp offers a 30-day money-back guarantee for all initial subscription purchases. If you cancel within 30 days of your starting date, you will receive a full refund. Cancellations made after 30 days will stop automatic renewal, but no pro-rated refunds are issued for the remainder of the billing period.

### 5. How do I contact Acme Corp customer support?
Our support team, supervised by Support Operations Manager Sarah Jenkins, is available Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM EST. You can reach us via email at support@acmecorp.io or by calling 1-800-555-0199. Enterprise customers receive 24/7 priority phone support.

### 6. What are the file attachment size limits?
File upload limits depend on your workspace tier:
* **Starter:** Up to 50MB per file (100GB total storage).
* **Business:** Up to 250MB per file (1TB total storage).
* **Enterprise:** Up to 1GB per file (unlimited storage).

### 7. What happens to my data after I cancel my account?
Following account cancellation, your workspace enters a 60-day grace period in read-only mode. On Day 61, under Data Retention Rule DR-402, all project data, files, and user records are permanently deleted from our primary servers and backups.

### 8. What are the rate limits for the developer API?
AcmeFlow Pro standard REST API access is limited to 1,000 request calls per minute per API key. Exceeding 1,200 calls in a rolling 60-second window triggers a `429 Too Many Requests` error response. Enterprise plans can request an elevated limit of 5,000 calls per minute.

### 9. When was the latest software update released?
Version 4.2 of AcmeFlow Pro was deployed on March 15, 2024. This update introduced the AI Task Summarizer feature, custom report export filters, and resolved performance latency on Kanban boards.

### 10. How do I adjust the number of paid seats on my account?
Account Owners can add or remove user seats under Settings > Billing > Manage Seats. Added seats are charged a pro-rated fee based on the remaining days in your billing cycle. Removed seats remain active until the next renewal date.

### 11. Which third-party tools integrate with AcmeFlow Pro?
AcmeFlow Pro supports native integrations with 42 external services, including Slack, Google Workspace, Microsoft Teams, Jira, and Salesforce. Integration settings can be configured by Workspace Administrators under Settings > Integrations.
