# Globex Health - Patient & Visitor FAQ

### 1. What are the general visiting hours and visitor policies at Globex Health facilities?
General visiting hours across all facilities—including St. Jude Campus in Springfield and Northside Medical Center in Riverdale—are from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily. Intensive Care Unit (ICU) visiting hours are restricted to 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Patients are allowed a maximum of 2 visitors at any given time. All visitors must be at least 12 years of age and must check in at the main lobby security desk with a valid government-issued photo ID to receive a visitor pass.

### 2. How do I schedule or cancel an outpatient appointment?
You can schedule appointments online via the MyGlobexChart portal (my.globexhealth.org) or by calling Central Scheduling at (555) 019-4321 between 7:00 AM and 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday. If you need to cancel or reschedule, you must provide at least 24 hours' notice prior to your appointment time. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice will incur a $45 no-show fee billed directly to the patient.

### 3. What are the parking options and rates at the St. Jude Campus?
Visitor parking is available in Garage A and Lot C. Parking in Garage A is free for the first 30 minutes, $3.00 per hour thereafter, up to a daily maximum cap of $12.00. Multi-day passes can be purchased at the main information desk for $10.00 per day. Valet parking is offered exclusively at the St. Jude Campus main entrance for a flat rate of $7.00 per day between 6:00 AM and 6:00 PM.

### 4. How can I request official paper copies of my medical records?
While digital records are accessible for free on MyGlobexChart, paper copies require a signed release form submitted to the Health Information Management (HIM) department. Processing takes 5 to 7 business days and carries a standard $15.00 administrative fulfillment fee. For status updates on your records request, call HIM directly at (555) 019-4402.

### 5. What should I bring with me for a planned inpatient hospital admission?
Please bring a government-issued photo ID, your current health insurance card, a copy of any Advance Directives or Living Wills, and a complete list of your current medications including dosages. Please leave all valuables, jewelry, and large sums of cash at home; Globex Health cannot assume responsibility for personal property kept in patient rooms.

### 6. What is the standard payment policy for Emergency Room visits?
Emergency departments at St. Jude Campus and Northside Medical Center are open 24/7. Patients evaluated at triage for non-emergent conditions will be required to pay their insurance plan's designated ER copayment (typically $250.00) at the time of check-in. Emergency medical care will never be delayed or denied based on inability to pay.

### 7. How do I request a prescription refill through the hospital pharmacy?
Prescription refill requests require 48 hours' advanced notice. Requests can be submitted electronically through MyGlobexChart or by calling the Globex Outpatient Pharmacy direct line at (555) 014-8833. Pharmacy operating hours are Monday through Saturday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM; the pharmacy is closed on Sundays and federal holidays.

### 8. What financial assistance programs are available for uninsured patients?
Globex Health offers financial aid to qualified patients residing in our six-county regional service area whose household income is at or below 300% of the Federal Poverty Level (FPL). To apply, submit a completed Financial Assistance Application along with your last two federal tax returns within 240 days from the date of your first post-discharge billing statement. Contact Financial Counseling at (800) 555-0199 for assistance.

### 9. When are hospital bills due, and is there an early payment incentive?
Billing statements are issued monthly and full payment is due within 30 days of the statement date. Globex Health offers a 15% prompt-pay discount on remaining out-of-pocket balances if the invoice is settled in full within 10 calendar days of issuance. Payments can be submitted at my.globexhealth.org or by calling (800) 555-0199.

### 10. Are telehealth consultations available at Pine Creek Outpatient Clinic?
Yes, Pine Creek Outpatient Clinic in Apex offers virtual appointments for established primary care patients on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Telehealth visits must be booked at least 48 hours in advance through MyGlobexChart or by calling the Pine Creek reception desk at (555) 012-7700.
