### 1. Q3 2024 Financial Performance Review
Sarah Jenkins (CFO) presented the consolidated third-quarter financial report. 

* **Q3 Revenue:** $412.5 million, reflecting a 3.2% year-over-year decline compared to Q3 2023 ($426.1 million).
* **YTD Revenue:** $1.24 billion.
* **Operating Margin:** -1.8% ($7.4 million net operating loss for Q3), primarily driven by elevated contract nursing expenses ($28.4 million) and bad debt write-offs ($19.1 million).
* **Adjusted EBITDA:** $31.8 million (down from $44.2 million in Q3 2023).
* **Cash Reserves:** Unrestricted cash on hand stands at $84.2 million as of September 30, 2024.

### 2. Project Horizon: Planned Acquisition of Meridian Surgical Centers
CEO Marcus Vance detailed the negotiated terms for acquiring **Meridian Surgical Centers LLC**, a network of six ambulatory surgical facilities across the regional area.

* **Agreed Purchase Price:** $87.5 million total enterprise value ($60.0 million cash, $27.5 million assumed debt).
* **Target Closing Date:** March 15, 2025.
* **Financial Impact:** Project Horizon is projected to add $54.0 million in annual top-line revenue and generate $8.2 million in run-rate EBITDA synergies within 18 months of closing.
* **Governance & Governance Rules:**
  1. Acquisition approval passed 7-1 (Arthur Gallagher voting nay, citing valuation concerns).
  2. Transaction is strictly contingent on securing a $65.0 million amended credit facility from First Federal Bank by December 15, 2024.
  3. Deal requires regulatory antitrust clearance under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Act prior to February 1, 2025.

### 3. Operational Realignment and Workforce Reduction (Project Efficiency)
Elena Rostova (VP of HR) presented the board-mandated cost-reduction strategy, titled **Project Efficiency**, aimed at restoring positive operating margins before Q2 2025.

* **Target Reduction:** Elimination of 380 Full-Time Equivalents (FTEs), representing approximately 4.5% of Globex Health's total workforce (8,400 FTEs).
* **Targeted Departments:** Corporate administration (160 FTEs), middle management across all five regional hospital sites (110 FTEs), and centralized IT/billing services (110 FTEs).
* **Care Exemption Mandate:** Bedside nursing, emergency department clinical staff, and active surgical personnel are strictly exempt from reductions under Board Resolution 2024-09.
* **Execution Timeline:**
  * **Notification Date:** November 12, 2024.
  * **Effective Separation Date:** December 31, 2024.
* **Severance & Benefit Rules:**
  1. *Non-exempt staff:* 2 weeks of base pay per full year of service (minimum 4 weeks, maximum 20 weeks).
  2. *Exempt/Management staff:* 3 weeks of base pay per full year of service (minimum 6 weeks, maximum 32 weeks).
  3. *Health Benefits:* Globex Health will fully subsidize COBRA premiums for 90 calendar days post-separation.
  4. *Non-Solicitation Rule:* Senior Directors and VPs receiving severance must sign a binding 12-month non-solicitation agreement to receive the final severance installment.
* **Financial Impact:** One-time severance and restructuring charge estimated at $14.2 million, to be recognized in Q4 2024. Net annual savings projected at $36.5 million starting in FY2025.
