### 1. Overview & Compensation Philosophy
Globex Health System manages compensation across its four regional sites: St. Jude Main Campus, Riverdale Community Hospital, Oakridge Specialty Center, and Valley Care Clinics. Base salaries are reviewed annually against regional healthcare benchmarks. Annual Incentive Plan (AIP) bonuses are calculated based on individual performance evaluations, fiscal year hospital operating margin goals, and Press Ganey patient satisfaction scores.

### 3. Annual Incentive Plan (AIP) Rules & Eligibility

1. **Eligibility Cutoff Date:** Employees must be hired into an eligible position on or before **September 30, 2024**, to qualify for the 2024 AIP cycle (paid on **March 15, 2025**). Staff hired between **July 1, 2024, and September 30, 2024**, will receive a prorated payout calculated at 50% of the calculated award.
2. **Performance Rating Multiplier:**
   * **Unsatisfactory (Rating 1):** 0% payout multiplier.
   * **Developing (Rating 2):** 50% payout multiplier.
   * **Proficient (Rating 3):** 100% payout multiplier.
   * **Exceeds Expectations (Rating 4):** 120% payout multiplier.
   * **Outstanding (Rating 5):** 140% payout multiplier.
3. **Network Gatekeeper Metric:** If Globex Health overall patient satisfaction falls below **84.5%** for Q1–Q4, total AIP funding for Levels L50 through L70 is automatically capped at **75% of target**, regardless of individual performance ratings.

### 4. Shift Differentials & Specialty Retention Rules

* **Night Shift Premium:** L20 and L30 staff working scheduled shifts between 19:00 and 07:00 receive a **$4.75/hour** shift differential.
* **Weekend Premium:** L20 and L30 staff working between 19:00 Friday and 07:00 Monday receive a **$3.25/hour** shift differential (stackable with Night Shift Premium).
* **ICU/ER Specialty Retention Stipend:** L30 RNs continuously assigned to Intensive Care or Emergency Departments with at least 24 months of service as of **November 1, 2024**, are eligible for a one-time **$6,500 retention bonus**, payable on **December 13, 2024**. Recipients must sign a 12-month service commitment agreement managed by HR Director David Miller.
