# Globex Health Employee Handbook
**Document Control ID:** GH-HR-2024-v3  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Approved by:** Sarah Jenkins, Chief Human Resources Officer  

## Section 1: Working Hours & Shift Schedules
Globex Health operates 24/7/365 to deliver continuous patient care across our four regional hospital facilities. 

* **Clinical Staff Shifts:** Operating on a 12-hour rotation schedule. Day Shift runs from 07:00 to 19:30; Night Shift runs from 19:00 to 07:30. Each 12.5-hour shift includes a mandatory 30-minute unpaid meal break and two 15-minute paid rest breaks.
* **Administrative Staff:** Standard office hours are 08:30 to 17:00, Monday through Friday, with a 60-minute unpaid lunch break.
* **Attendance & Punctuality:** Staff must clock in using the Kronos portal within a 7-minute grace window (e.g., 06:53 to 07:07 for a 07:00 shift). Accumulating three (3) unexcused tardies within any rolling 30-day period will result in a Level 1 Written Warning issued by Shift Supervisor Dave Miller (Clinical) or Karen Vance (Administrative).

## Section 2: Leave Policy & Time Off
* **Paid Time Off (PTO) Accrual:** Full-time employees in years 1–3 of employment accrue PTO at a rate of 4.61 hours per 80-hour pay period (totaling up to 120 hours annually). 
* **PTO Rollover Limit:** A maximum of 40 unused PTO hours may be rolled over into the next calendar year. Any excess balance above 40 hours is automatically forfeited on December 31 at 23:59 PST.
* **Unplanned Absences:** For unexpected sick leave, staff must log the absence in the automated call-in portal (1-800-555-0199) and inform their direct supervisor at least four (4) hours prior to the scheduled shift start time.
* **Bereavement Leave:** Full-time employees are eligible for up to five (5) consecutive paid days for the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling) and up to two (2) paid days for extended family.

## Section 3: Remote Work & Telecommuting
* **Eligibility:** Remote work is strictly limited to non-clinical, Tier 2 administrative staff and above. Approval must be granted in writing by Department Director Marcus Thorne.
* **Hybrid Limits:** Approved employees may work remotely up to two (2) days per workweek (typically Tuesdays and Thursdays). Full-time remote arrangements require executive committee sign-off.
* **Security Requirements:** Employees working off-site must connect through the Globex VPN (v7.2) and complete mandatory CyberShield security training by the 15th day of each quarter. Conducting patient-related business on personal devices is strictly prohibited.

## Section 4: Code of Conduct & Workplace Standards
* **HIPAA & Patient Privacy:** Accessing Patient Health Information (PHI) without a direct treatment or operational need will result in immediate termination and referral to regulatory authorities.
* **Dress Code:** Clinical personnel must wear Navy Blue scrubs featuring the embroidered Globex logo on the left chest. Administrative personnel must adhere to business casual attire. Facial piercings are restricted to a single nose stud not exceeding 2mm in diameter.
* **Substance Policy:** Globex Health maintains a zero-tolerance substance policy. Mandatory random drug screenings are administered under the direction of Dr. Robert Chen, Lead Occupational Health Officer.
